## Project Alpha: The Global Economic Infrastructure Report

## 阿尔法项目：全球经济基础设施报告

This document will describe the current design and development of our financial systems.

本文将描述我们金融系统的当前设计与开发。

DIRECTOR: We must follow each economic effect during this difficult period.

主管：在这一困难时期，我们必须关注每一项经济影响。

We need to decide if the current condition allows for further effort.

我们需要决定当前的状况是否允许进一步的努力。

SARAH: I feel that the economic education in each department is not enough.

莎拉：我觉得各部门的经济教育还不够。

The final decision depends on the firm financial base we build today.

最终决定取决于我们今天建立的稳固金融基础。

DIRECTOR: I agree that the development of each foot of the building must continue.

主管：我同意大楼每一英尺的开发必须继续。

Every event during the first forty or fifty days will be critical.

前四十或五十天内的每一次事件都至关重要。

CHRIS: Doesn't the foreign economy effect our local firm?

克里斯：国外经济难道不影响我们的本地公司吗？

DIRECTOR: It does, but we must find a different way to direct our effort.

主管：确实如此，但我们必须找到一种不同的方式来引导我们的努力。

Each department should draw a clear plan for the coming year.

每个部门都应该为来年画出一份清晰的计划。

SARAH: We have eighteen or eighty examples of how a firm can fail.

莎拉：关于一家公司如何失败，我们有十八或八十个例子。

The film in the education center will describe the former development.

教育中心的影片将描述之前的开发情况。

DIRECTOR: Follow the money and you will find the family of firms behind the project.

主管：追踪资金，你会发现项目背后的公司家族。

During the election, the effort to change the economy was ever present.

在选举期间，改变经济的努力一直存在。

CHRIS: I fear the effect of the current financial situation on our east office.

克里斯：我担心当前的财务状况对我们东部办公室的影响。

DIRECTOR: Even if we fail, we must experience each stage of the development.

主管：即使我们失败了，我们也必须经历开发的每个阶段。

Everything we design must be for the benefit of the family and the country.

我们设计的每样东西都必须是为了家庭和国家的利益。

SARAH: Eleven directors from different firms will follow our lead.

莎拉：来自不同公司的十一名主管将追随我们的领导。

Else, the entire economic system will fall into a difficult state.

否则，整个经济系统将陷入困难状态。

DIRECTOR: We must direct all available aid to the financial department.

主管：我们必须将所有可用的援助直接拨给财务部门。

Each foot of progress we make helps us stay behind the lead.

我们取得的每一英尺进步都帮助我们保持领先（注：此处语境指稳步前进）。

CHRIS: Is it enough to just follow the current design?

克里斯：仅仅遵循当前的设计就足够了吗？

DIRECTOR: No, we must develop a final plan that covers every event.

主管：不，我们必须制定一个涵盖每一项事件的最终计划。

Everything in the firm must follow the director's direct orders.

公司里的一切都必须遵循主管的直接命令。

SARAH: The effort to feel the market effect is early but necessary.

莎拉：感受市场影响的努力虽然尚早，但很有必要。

We must find the first sign of economic change in the east.

我们必须在东方寻找经济变化的第一个迹象。

DIRECTOR: During the next fifty days, every eye will be on our financial firm.

主管：在接下来的五十天里，每一只眼睛都会盯着我们的金融公司。

Each example of success will help the education of the entire department.

每一个成功的例子都将有助于整个部门的教育。

CHRIS: If we don't follow the plan, the former director will be disappointed.

克里斯：如果我们不遵循计划，前任主管会感到失望。

DIRECTOR: Eight or eighteen different factors can effect the final result.

主管：八个或十八个不同的因素会影响最终结果。

We must feel the fear but continue our effort anyway.

我们必须感受恐惧，但无论如何都要继续我们的努力。
